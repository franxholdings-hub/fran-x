# FRAN-X 

BUILD FRAN-X HOLDINGS — OFFICIAL CORPORATE BUSINESS & SERVICES PLATFORM



Build a premium, modern, enterprise-grade website and business services platform for FRAN-X Holdings, a diversified business group founded by Francis Ejimkeonye, Founder & CEO.



This must NOT look like a simple freelancer portfolio.



It should feel like a serious, ambitious, technology-driven corporate group, business solutions company, consulting firm, digital agency, and business opportunity platform.



The platform should allow visitors to:



1. Learn about FRAN-X Holdings

2. Explore all current and future FRAN-X companies

3. Explore FRAN-X services

4. Search for a service

5. Request a service

6. Request FRAN-X to build a website

7. Request FRAN-X to build a mobile application

8. Submit business opportunities

9. Submit partnership opportunities

10. Submit investment opportunities

11. Contact FRAN-X directly

12. Contact FRAN-X through WhatsApp

13. Eventually create an account and track their projects through a client portal
